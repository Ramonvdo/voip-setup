# Disclaimer

This project provisions a phone line. It does not make what you do with that line lawful.

**Outbound calling is regulated, and the rules differ by country, by US state, and by whether
you are calling a business or a consumer.** Before you dial anyone, the responsibility for
the following is yours:

- **Do-not-call rules.** Calls to consumers are restricted in most jurisdictions, with
  registries you may be required to check and penalties per call for ignoring them.
  Business-to-business calling is treated differently in many places, but "differently" is
  not "exempt".
- **Automated dialling.** Dialling equipment that places calls without a human is regulated
  far more tightly than a person dialling one number at a time, particularly to mobiles.
- **Permitted hours,** which follow the time zone of the person being called, not yours.
- **Recording.** Some places require every party to consent, some only one. Recording is off
  by default here; if you switch it on, announce it.
- **Caller ID.** Transmitting a number you do not control is illegal in the US under the
  Truth in Caller ID Act, and equivalents exist elsewhere. This tool sets the caller ID to a
  number your own account owns, which is the only arrangement it supports.
- **Registration.** Some jurisdictions require telemarketers to register or hold a licence,
  regardless of call volume.

The scripts here spend real money on your carrier account: they buy a phone number and place
calls at your carrier's rates. The spending limits they set are a guardrail, not a guarantee.
